About us

About Rosie-Andre Consulting

Rosie-Andre Consulting is a professional management and institutional consulting firm providing strategic advisory, organisational development, institutional reform, policy development, capacity building, training and specialised consultancy services. We work with public institutions, private organisations and other stakeholders to diagnose challenges, develop practical solutions, strengthen systems and people, and improve organisational performance.
