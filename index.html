About the group

One group, five companies

The Road Group of Companies (TRGC) is the parent company of five businesses: Favorite Homes Limited, Benny Andrew's Security, Iykchiz Company, Rose-Andrew Solicitors and Rosie-Andre Consulting. Each company runs independently under its own name and site, and all share the group's commitment to dependable, professional service.

Our mission

To pave the right path to getting every job done right, by building dependable companies that deliver quality work and service.

Our vision

To be the trusted road that leads people and organisations to the right means of getting things done, leading where the future beckons.
